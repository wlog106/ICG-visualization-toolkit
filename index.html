ICG Visualization Toolkit

Interactive visualizations for Introduction to Computer Graphics: viewing, projection, lighting, and the pipeline. Each tool is a standalone HTML page.

Overview

Pipeline Exam Map

Every pipeline stage, from model transform to frame buffer, with the exam topics that belong to it.

 Ch3 · Viewing

Projection Explorer

A cube, its projectors, and the projection plane in 3D, next to the resulting image. Covers multiview, axonometric, oblique, and 1–3 point perspective.

 Ch4 · Shading

Phong Vectors

n, l, r, v at a surface point, and which of them the ambient, diffuse, and specular terms use.

 Ch4 · Shading

Phong Sphere Lab

Toggle each reflection term, tune k and α, and compare flat, Gouraud, and Phong shading on a triangle mesh.

 Ch2 · Transformations

Orbit Animation Builder

Build an orbiting model's matrix one factor at a time, compare it against common wrong orders, attach a second model, and view the scene through its eye.

 Ch3 · Viewing

Normalized View Volume

Morph the orthographic box, oblique parallelepiped, or perspective frustum into the same 2×2×2 cube, and watch depth spacing and object sizes change.
