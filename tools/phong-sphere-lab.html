Phong Sphere Lab

A triangle-mesh sphere lit by the Phong reflection model. Switch terms on and off, and compare where the formula runs: per triangle (flat), per vertex (Gouraud), or per pixel (Phong shading).

Shading method (where the formula runs)

Things to try

  • Turn on one term at a time. Ambient alone gives a flat disc, diffuse alone a lit side fading to dark, specular alone a white dot.
  • Drag α from 200 down to 5 and watch the highlight go from a sharp metal glint to a broad plastic sheen.
  • Set Triangles to about 5 and turn on Show triangles. Gouraud blurs or loses a highlight that falls inside a triangle. Phong shading keeps it.
  • Raise Triangles to about 20. All three methods converge, because tiny triangles make "per triangle" close to "per pixel".

I = k_a·I_a + k_d·I_d·max(l·n,0) + k_s·I_s·max(r·v,0)^α, with r = 2(l·n)n − l. The viewer looks along −z (orthographic), and the light is directional.
