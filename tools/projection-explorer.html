Projection explorer

Every projection name answers two questions: how the projectors travel, and how the object is turned relative to the plane. Click through the types and watch which one changes.

Principal directions and faces

  • Principal directions: the object's own three edge directions (width x, height y, depth z).
  • Principal faces: the flat faces perpendicular to those directions (front/back, left/right, top/bottom).

The two questions

  • Projectors? Converge at a COP → perspective. Parallel and ⟂ → orthographic. Parallel and slanted → oblique.
  • Orientation? Face ∥ plane → multiview. Tilted: 3 equal angles iso, 2 di, 0 tri. Perspective: one vanishing point per principal direction not ∥ plane.
