Phong Vectors

The four vectors at a surface point, and which of them each term of the Phong reflection model uses.

Reading each term

Ambient asks whether there's any light at all. It's a constant that stands in for light bounced around the room, so it uses no vectors.

Diffuse asks whether the point faces the light. Brightness falls off as cos θ = l·n. It ignores the viewer, so a matte surface looks the same from anywhere.

Specular asks whether the mirror reflection r heads into the eye. It's brightest when φ is small. The exponent α sets the highlight size. This is the only term that changes when the viewer moves.
